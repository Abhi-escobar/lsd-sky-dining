Our Story

A Night Above
The City

High above Jamshedpur's skyline, LSD — Lounge & Sky Dining exists at the intersection of atmosphere and indulgence. Step beneath our iconic bamboo canopy threaded with wicker lanterns, let the warm golden glow of neon wash over you, and settle into a world designed for moments that matter.

Whether you're arriving for a romantic dinner under an open sky, a family celebration in our festive indoor lounge, or a late-night escape with friends — every corner of LSD tells a story of warmth, craft, and curated cuisine. From the smokiness of Hyderabadi dum to the crispness of South Indian mornings and the bold umami of Chinese stir-fries, our kitchen is a love letter to three great culinary traditions.
